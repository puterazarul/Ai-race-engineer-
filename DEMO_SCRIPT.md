# 60-second demo script
1. "This is AI Race Engineer, a dashboard that analyzes racing telemetry. The data is simulated, since I don't own a racing sim yet."
2. Top row: total laps, fastest lap, average lap time and max speed, all calculated with Pandas.
3. Pick two laps. "The app shows which lap was faster and by how much."
4. Scroll the graphs: "Speed, throttle and brake against distance, so you can see where one lap gains or loses time."
5. Race Engineer Insights: "These are rule-based. Python measures corner speed, braking and throttle, finds the sector where the slower lap lost the most time, and writes a recommendation from those numbers."
6. Close: "Next step is importing real sim telemetry."
